FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer emergency plumbing service?

Yes. We answer the phone 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. For burst pipes and major leaks, shut off your main water valve and call us right away.

Will I get a price before the work starts?

Always. After your plumber diagnoses the problem, you'll receive a written, fixed price. We don't start until you approve it.

Are your plumbers licensed and insured?

Yes. Every technician is licensed, background-checked and covered by our liability insurance and bonding.

Do you guarantee your work?

All workmanship comes with a one-year warranty. If something we installed or repaired fails within a year, we'll come back and make it right.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept major credit cards, checks and bank transfer. Financing is available on larger projects such as water heater replacements and re-pipes.
