About us

A family-run crew your neighbours already call.

BlueRidge started with one van and a simple rule: show up on time, explain the problem in plain language, and never charge for work you don't need. Today we are a team of 24 licensed plumbers, apprentices and dispatchers serving homes, restaurants, clinics and property managers.

Every technician is background-checked, drug-tested and trained in-house, and every job is photographed and documented so you always know what was done.
